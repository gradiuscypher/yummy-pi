# Evidence ledger

Use a compact ledger for consequential research. This is a reasoning aid, not an automated claim-verification tool.

| Claim / figure | Source owner and URL | Service / report scope | Price status | Unit and exclusions | Evidence quality / uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Example: platform starts at $7,500 | Official vendor pricing page, URL | Software only, one framework | Published starting price | Annual subscription; CPA fee not established as included | Direct page evidence; final quote and renewal unknown |
| Example: Type I audit $5k–$20k | Software vendor cost guide, URL | Type I audit only; company profile unspecified | Published market estimate, not an offer | Per audit; readiness excluded | Undisclosed sample/method; corroborate with CPA sources |
| Example: engineering 160 hours | Analyst estimate informed by scope sources, URLs | Hands-on implementation for stated environment | Planning assumption, not a market price | Hours; documentation and audit support separate | Bottom-up judgment; actual gaps may change it |

Record the page's publication/update dates if available, the retrieval date, and whether full relevant content was read. Search snippets alone should remain marked **discovery-only**.

## Comparison checks

Before comparing two numbers, ask:

- Are the services and deliverables actually the same?
- Is this implementation or advice about implementation?
- Is a fixed fee conditional on readiness, headcount, a platform subscription, or restricted scope?
- Is the number a starting price, an average with an identified sample, a vendor estimate, or a quote?
- Does a bundle already include readiness, software, testing, or the independent auditor?
- Is internal labor valued separately from external cash?

## Closing a research task

Summarize:

1. Claims established by primary sources.
2. Estimates/inferences and their assumptions.
3. Conflicting or weak claims not used as firm evidence.
4. Gaps requiring more research versus a quote or an interview.
5. Useful sources cited, distinct providers actually attempted, and task-local diagnostic deltas if relevant.

Do not equate more searches with more confidence. Do not label a planning estimate a measured market benchmark.
